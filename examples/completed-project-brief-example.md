# Completed Project Brief Example

This fictional example shows the amount of detail needed to direct the work without turning the brief into a long requirements document.

## Project

- **Project name:** Northline Operations
- **Human owner:** Maya Patel
- **Working repository:** `northline-operations-site`
- **Target release:** 18 October 2026

## Who the site is for

Founders of UK service businesses with 15–60 employees whose delivery work has outgrown informal processes.

## What they should do

Book a 30-minute fit call to decide whether a six-week operations review is appropriate.

## Core claim

Northline helps growing service businesses make delivery work visible, repeatable and easier to manage without adding unnecessary process.

## Evidence

- Maya has led operations teams in three service businesses; approved career record held in the source of truth.
- The six-week review has been used with seven clients; only the approved aggregate wording may be public.
- Two client quotations are approved for the Results page; the signed permissions are stored outside the repository.

## Tone

- **Should feel:** calm, specific, experienced, direct.
- **Should not feel:** corporate, urgent, motivational or software-led.
- **Example that fits:** “See where delivery work is getting stuck, then decide what to change.”
- **Example that does not:** “Become the business you were meant to be.”

## What the site is not doing

- It is not selling implementation retainers.
- It is not aimed at large enterprises or solo businesses.
- It is not claiming guaranteed savings or growth.
- It is not collecting detailed operational data through a public form.

## Required pages and page jobs

| Page | Who arrives here | One job | Primary action | What belongs elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Referred founder | Establish fit and credibility | View the review | Full method detail |
| Operations review | Interested founder | Explain the six-week engagement | Book a fit call | General biography |
| Results | Evidence-seeking buyer | Show approved outcomes and quotations | Book a fit call | Service process |
| About | Buyer checking experience | Establish relevant operating experience | View the review | Full case studies |
| Contact | Ready prospect | Set expectations for the first conversation | Book a fit call | Long qualification form |

## Approved visual direction

- **Overall direction:** restrained editorial layout with strong headings and real working-session photography.
- **References and what to take from each:** annual reports for spacing and hierarchy; workshop manuals for practical diagrams. Do not copy branding or layouts.
- **Approved colours:** ink `#13212B`, paper `#F7F4EE`, muted blue `#557A8A`, rust `#A34B2A` for warnings only.
- **Approved typography:** system sans serif for interface text; approved serif for long quotations.
- **Image approach:** real photographs supplied by the owner; no generated office imagery.
- **Settled components:** header, footer, primary button and evidence card.
- **Deliberate exceptions:** the Results page may use denser evidence cards than other pages because comparison is its job.

## Constraints

- Static site on the existing host.
- No CMS in the first release.
- Calendly is the only third-party form or scheduling script.
- Consent and analytics require review before production.
- All client names remain anonymous unless separate written approval is recorded.

## Success criteria

- [x] A referred founder can identify the audience and offer from the first screen.
- [x] The operations review page explains the six-week engagement without promising an outcome.
- [x] Every public result is traceable to an approved source.
- [x] The fit-call action uses the same wording and destination across pages.
- [x] Desktop and mobile visual review is complete.
- [x] Analytics, consent and rollback checks pass before release.
